# Indian IT Companies Database

## Overview
This document catalogs IT companies across major Indian tech hubs for JobScout integration.
Each company is categorized by their hiring platform to determine scraping strategy.

---

## Company Categories by Hiring Platform

### Category A: Public ATS APIs (Easy Integration)
Companies using Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby with public APIs.

### Category B: Indian ATS Platforms (Medium Effort)
Companies using Freshteam, Keka, Darwinbox, Zoho Recruit - may have APIs.

### Category C: Custom Career Portals (High Effort)
Companies with custom-built career pages - requires web scraping.

### Category D: LinkedIn/Naukri Only (Skip)
Companies posting only on job boards - not feasible to scrape.
