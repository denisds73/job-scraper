This document outlines the Indian IT job market landscape, ATS platforms used by Indian companies, major tech hubs, salary benchmarks, and the complete list of changes required to pivot JobScout to India-only.

## 5. Currency & Formatting

### INR Formatting Rules
- Symbol: ₹ (U+20B9)
- Grouping: Indian system (lakhs, crores)
  - 10,00,000 = 10 Lakh
  - 1,00,00,000 = 1 Crore
- Common abbreviations:
  - LPA = Lakhs Per Annum
  - L = Lakh (100,000)
  - Cr = Crore (10,000,000)

### Display Examples
- ₹15L - ₹25L/year
- ₹15,00,000 - ₹25,00,000
- 15-25 LPA
